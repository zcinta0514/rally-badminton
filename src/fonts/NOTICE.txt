Self-hosted fonts for RALLY

Rally Display and Rally UI are renamed WOFF2 subsets of Noto Sans SC,
weight axis 400--650. Display contains the two Chinese title glyphs.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc

Rally Numerals is a renamed ASCII subset of Barlow Condensed SemiBold.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed
Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Full license: LICENSE-Barlow.txt
Source SHA256: 7b619d14bc2327509a9ef32b0890f709626f7ecc9ff61191c2a4314c5499d2d9

Subsets preserve original glyph outlines. Unknown player-name characters use
system fallback. No external font service is contacted at runtime.
Rebuild: scripts/prepare-lobby-fonts.py NotoSansSC.ttf BarlowCondensed-SemiBold.ttf
Requires fonttools and brotli. Source TTF files are development inputs only.
