Regular and Teen bodies shown in the full pack's promotion are not present.

RALLY adaptation:
The character's head, eyebrows, eyes, exposed limbs and parted hair are
adapted locally in Blender. Body proportions are narrowed and geometry/weights
are adjusted to the existing RALLY limb lengths and an 18-bone semantic rig.
The project adds its own sports jersey details, retains its own neck, hands, grip,
shorts and shoes, and uses flat-colour materials instead of upstream textures.
The resulting athlete.glb has no external animation clips or texture files.
Gameplay poses and exact contact timing remain controlled by RALLY game code.
No AI-generated asset or external asset-processing upload is used in this step.
